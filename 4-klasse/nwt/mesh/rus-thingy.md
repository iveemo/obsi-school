I broke off the stick's sides to save space.
It's glued into the frame. After that, solder the wires. Ground is connected via a 100-300 ohm resistor. Left/right contacts are on 45 and 42, and the press contact is on 48.
Settings are made in the "canned" module settings. After this, the rocker switch functions as a menu navigation.
