The trench gun, as it was called, was a short-barreled [pump action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pump_action "Pump action") shotgun loaded with 6 rounds containing [antimony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antimony "Antimony")-hardened [00 buckshot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckshot "Buckshot") (12 gauge), and equipped with a bayonet

conventional shotguns (back then) were mainly used for sporting/hunting purposes

Key differences:
	shorter barrel
	higher round capabilities
	heat shield
	bayonet lug
	slam fire (only some models)

Primary example: Winchester Model 1897
